Triangulator wire formats

One sampler (plus an optional socket sampler) sends fixed-size binary datagrams over UDP to the collector. Every datagram starts with a 4-byte magic that says what it is. Big samples are split into parts (chunks) that share a header and are put back together by the collector. Source of truth: common/wire.hpp, common/resource_wire.hpp, common/memory_wire.hpp, socket_sampler/protocol.hpp.

      Every datagram is kept under 1400 bytes (static_assert) so it fits one 1500-byte Ethernet MTU without IP fragmentation. “Parts” are our own application-level split; losing one part never blocks the others. No retransmission, no ACKs. TMON, TRES and TVMA share a session (random u64 per sampler start) so the collector can match streams and reject late datagrams from an old session.
