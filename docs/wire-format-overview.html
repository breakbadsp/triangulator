Triangulator wire formats

The sampler and an optional socket sampler send binary datagrams over UDP to the collector. Every datagram starts with a 4-byte magic that identifies the format. Large samples are split into parts (chunks) that share sample identifiers and are put back together by the collector. Source of truth: common/wire.hpp, common/resource_wire.hpp, common/memory_wire.hpp, socket_sampler/protocol.hpp.

Hover a datagram for its size, and click one in flight to drop it. Raise packet loss to watch a sample arrive incomplete. TMON uses received threads. TRES needs its summary.       Every datagram is at most 1400 bytes (static_assert) so it fits a normal 1500-byte IP MTU without IP fragmentation. A smaller path MTU can still cause fragmentation or loss. “Parts” are our own application-level split; losing one part never blocks the others. No retransmission, no ACKs. TMON, TRES and TVMA share a session (random u64 per sampler session) so the collector can match streams and reject late datagrams from an old session.
