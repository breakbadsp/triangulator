States come from the thread's scheduler state and kernel wait channel (/proc/<tid>/wchan), readable without extra privileges; futex waits cannot be split into lock, condition or timed idle. CPU alerts open after a thread stays above the threshold for the configured duration (default 5 seconds). Read/write rates count bytes moved by read- and write-family syscalls, including sockets. Timelines show sampled state mixes, not exact execution. CPU is per thread, relative to one core.
